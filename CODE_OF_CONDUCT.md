# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Empathy**: Demonstrating kindness and understanding toward others
- **Respect**: Valuing different perspectives, experiences, and opinions
- **Constructive feedback**: Giving and gracefully accepting feedback
- **Accountability**: Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- **Inclusivity**: Focusing on what's best for the overall community
- **Collaboration**: Working together toward common goals

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- **Harassment**: Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal/political attacks
- **Discrimination**: Discriminatory jokes, language, or actions
- **Sexual content**: Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- **Doxxing**: Publishing private information without consent
- **Spam/Trolling**: Off-topic, repetitive, or disruptive comments
- **Threats**: Physical or psychological threats of harm
- **Unprofessional conduct**: Any behavior inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

- GitHub repository (issues, PRs, discussions, wiki)
- GitHub Actions workflows
- Related communication channels (Discord, Slack, email)
- Events (virtual or in-person) organized by the project

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders at **eshan@hablu-programmer.com**.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

### Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines:

#### 1. Correction
**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence**: Private, written warning with clarity on the violation and why it was inappropriate. Public apology may be requested.

#### 2. Warning
**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: Warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with people involved for a specified period. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces and external channels.

#### 3. Temporary Ban
**Community Impact**: Serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: Temporary ban from any interaction or communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with people involved during this period.

#### 4. Permanent Ban
**Community Impact**: Demonstrated pattern of violation, including harassment or aggression toward individuals or groups.

**Consequence**: Permanent ban from all public interaction within the community.
