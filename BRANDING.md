# Branding and Trademarks

Support Library is distributed under the MIT License. The license requires preservation of its copyright and license notice in copies or substantial portions of the software. It does **not** require a "Powered by Hablu Programmer" label, footer, link, or other attribution in the user interface.

## Forks and distributions

You may replace the application name, logo, colors, and copy in a fork. If a distribution keeps the original copyright notice, that notice must remain accurate and the MIT license text must remain included as required by `LICENSE`.

Brand removal does not remove the MIT notice, and keeping the MIT notice does not grant trademark rights.

## Trademark guidance

"Hablu Programmer", "Hablu Support Library", and the Hablu logo identify the original project and organization. The MIT software license does not grant permission to use those names or logos to imply sponsorship, endorsement, or official status.

For a public fork:

- Choose a distinct product name and replace `public/logo.png` unless the fork is clearly presented as unofficial.
- Do not describe a modified distribution as an official Hablu Programmer release.
- Factual statements such as "based on Hablu Support Library" are acceptable when they are not misleading.
- Request written permission before using Hablu marks in promotion, domain names, or a way that suggests endorsement.

## Project styling

The upstream interface currently uses a cyan-to-violet gradient (`#06b6d4` to `#8b5cf6`), slate dark surfaces, Inter typography, and `public/logo.png`. These are implementation details, not mandatory conditions of the MIT license.
